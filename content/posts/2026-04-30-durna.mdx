> Sen Watansyz hasap etme gonup-göçen durnany...

Gurbannazar Ezizow, on the migrating crane — the *durna* that carries no
passport and knows no borders. Lines I come back to, far from home. The full
poem is on [serpay](https://serpay.penjire.com/p/gurbannazar-ezizow/gozum-dusdi).
